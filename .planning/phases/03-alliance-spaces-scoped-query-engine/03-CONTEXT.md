## Phase Boundary

Phase 3 delivers private alliance workspaces, multi-model airport assignment registries, audience-scoped query engines (`public` vs `alliance_only`), 4-tier role-based access control (Owner, Admin, Member, Guest), and background workers (`pg-boss`) for automated listing escalation and expiration.

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### 1. Airport Assignment & Route Matrix
- **D-01 (Flexible Multi-Hub Registration):** Alliance members can register between 1 and 3 airports that they actively operate in World of Airports. This supports specialized hub controllers in large alliances as well as broad coverage in smaller groups.
- **D-02 (Route Partner Priority Ping):** When an alliance member posts a flight matching an assigned partner's airport (e.g. PRG ➔ IAD), the system highlights the contract with partner priority tags and notifications, but keeps claiming open to the wider group after a configurable grace period.
- **D-03 (Interactive Airport Route Matrix):** The private operations workspace features a visual coverage matrix of all 10 official WoA hubs, indicating which members cover each airport and visually highlighting route gaps where flight turnarounds are needed.

### 2. Audience Scoping & Escalation Timers
- **D-04 (Creator Choice with Alliance Ceiling):** Requesters can toggle "Auto-escalate to public if unclaimed" with a timer (e.g., 2h, 4h, 6h), subject to the alliance leader's configured maximum escalation policy.
- **D-05 (Public Escalation Contract Nature):** Alliance members are predominantly plane *senders* due to their large fleets; external players and guests act as turnaround *hosts*. Once an alliance listing's private timer elapses without a member claim, it transitions into a standard open contract on the public marketplace so any community pilot can claim and host the aircraft.
- **D-06 (Background Worker):** PostgreSQL-backed `pg-boss` worker processes listing timers, automatically escalating eligible alliance listings to the public board and archiving stale listings (24h expiry for unclaimed listings, 4h for unconfirmed claims).

### 3. Membership Model & Multi-Alliance Policy
- **D-07 (Single Primary Alliance Member, Multi-Guest):** A pilot can only be a full **Member**, **Admin**, or **Owner** of **one primary alliance** at a time to prevent conflicting airport claims and split loyalties. However, a pilot can register as a **Guest / Affiliate** of multiple alliances to receive host requests and handle incoming fleet turnarounds.
- **D-08 (Configurable Onboarding Mode):** Alliance leaders can configure their join mode:
  1. *Open Join*: Any verified pilot can join immediately.
  2. *Application Review*: Candidates submit a brief application note and are approved by an Admin/Owner.
  3. *Secret Invite Link*: Onboarding requires a secure tokenized invite URL generated by an Admin.
- **D-09 (4-Tier RBAC):** Enforces strict permissions across Owner, Admin, Member, and Guest/Visitor roles.

### 4. Dedicated Multi-Page Alliance UI Architecture
- **D-10 (Dedicated 3-View Architecture):** To uphold the zero-clutter policy, alliances are split into 3 spacious pages:
  1. `/alliances`: Discovery directory of alliances, filtering by airport model (Assigned, Flexible, Hybrid), active member counts, and recruitment status.
  2. `/alliances/:slug`: Public alliance dossier and recruitment lounge with group overview, operating hubs, Discord link, and "Apply / Register as Guest Host" CTA.
  3. `/alliances/:slug/deck`: Private operations command deck with member-only dispatch board, interactive airport route matrix, member roster management, and admin policy controls.
- **D-11 (Design System Continuity):** Replicates the airport parchment palette (`#F0EBDE` canvas, `#FFFFFA` cards, `#C9C7BA` hairline borders, `#141A1F` tarmac black, `#1240C7` cobalt, `#A8C229` lime, and `#F25E24` orange).
